      What is real here. The index, the ranking, the staleness decay and the shared store are live — every viewer of this page sees the same inventory, and edits persist. The sensors are not. Sightings are simulated, and the scan above is ray-cast against the floor plan rather than measured — at the C1's real parameters, slowed from 10 Hz so the sweep is legible. Its occlusion is not faked. When a bench stands between the sensor and the target, the scope reports no line of sight, because a single-plane scanner genuinely cannot see past it. That is the argument in one frame: LiDAR gives geometry but cannot name an object, a camera names objects but places them poorly, and neither sees behind a box. Until both run together, an honest index reports when it last knew rather than pretending to know now.
